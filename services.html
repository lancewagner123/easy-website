Website building

A hosting-ready website for your business — here's how it works.

Build a website.

Whether it's a brand-new site or a long-overdue upgrade, every project starts the same way: answer our Intake Questionnaire, then send over your content — real photos, a description of your business, and the words you want people to read. That's all we need.

From there, we handle everything else: layout, engineering, responsive behavior, and polish. The result looks like the work in our portfolio — not a template with your name swapped in.

Everything that goes into your site

A mix of standard capabilities and custom builds — pulled from real work across our own projects, not a generic feature list.

  • Contact Form

    A way for visitors to reach you directly from the site.

  • Click-to-Call & Click-to-Email

    One-tap contact from any phone or device — no copying numbers by hand.

  • Cross-Device Testing

    Every page is captured and measured at desktop, tablet, and phone widths before it ships — not eyeballed, verified.

  • Optimized, Fast-Loading Images

    Every photo converted and resized for the web, so your site loads fast on any connection.

  • Reviews, Testimonials & Trust Badges

    Real customer reviews and licensing or accreditation badges, displayed to build trust before anyone picks up the phone.

  • Google Maps & Directions

    An interactive map and a one-click directions link, right where visitors expect it.

  • SEO & Structured Data

    Search-friendly titles, rich social share previews, and structured data that helps search engines understand your business.

  • Accessibility Built In

    Skip links, keyboard navigation, and screen-reader-friendly markup on every page.

  • Online Ordering Integration

    A direct link into your existing ordering platform, so customers can order and pay without leaving the flow.

  • Order & Inquiry Forms

    Purpose-built request forms — catering orders, reservations, quote requests — that route straight to you.

  • Photo Gallery & Carousel

    Show off real photos of your business, work, or products — a static grid or an auto-advancing slideshow, whichever fits your content.

  • Social Media Links

    Point visitors to your existing profiles and feeds.

  • Multi-Language Support

    Reach customers in more than one language, with their choice remembered on return visits.
