A hosting-ready website for your business — here's how it works.

Build a website.

Whether it's a brand-new site or a long-overdue upgrade, every project starts the same way: answer the Intake Questionnaire, then send over your content — real photos, a description of your business, and the words you want people to read. That's all it takes.

From there, everything else is handled — layout, responsive behavior, and polish, matched to what's provided. The result looks like the work in the portfolio — not a one-size-fits-all template.

Every site includes, as standard: cross-device testing at desktop, tablet, and phone widths; optimized, fast-loading images; SEO & structured data; and accessibility best practices. None of that is an upsell — it's the baseline.

What can be included in your site

On top of that baseline, any combination of these can be included, based on your content and your answers to the questionnaire.

  • Contact Form

    A way for visitors to reach you directly from the site.

  • Click-to-Call & Click-to-Email

    One-tap contact from any phone or device — no copying numbers by hand.

  • Reviews, Testimonials & Trust Badges

    Real customer reviews and licensing or accreditation badges, displayed to build trust before anyone picks up the phone.

  • Google Maps & Directions

    An interactive map and a one-click directions link, right where visitors expect it.

  • Photo Gallery & Carousel

    Show off real photos of your business, work, or products — a static grid or an auto-advancing slideshow, whichever fits your content.

  • Social Media Links

    Point visitors to your existing profiles and feeds.

  • Multi-Language Support

    Reach customers in more than one language, with their choice remembered on return visits.
